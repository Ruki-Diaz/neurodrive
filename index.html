02 / PIPELINE

SYSTEM ARCHITECTURE

CLICK ANY STATION TO INSPECT

01 Map

OSM STREETS

Real OpenStreetMap street geometries of the Melbourne CBD (Hoddle Grid) projected to metric coordinates (+x east, +y south). Road widths, speed limits (40 km/h), one-way Little streets, and tram tracks are indexed into a spatial hash grid.

02 Router

A* · 15 M WAYPOINTS

Road polylines split at intersections into a directed graph honouring one-way rules. A* computes the shortest path—such as Flinders St Station → Queen Victoria Market—placing route waypoints every 15 m.

03 Sensors

7 LIDAR RAYS

Each 4.5 m × 1.9 m vehicle casts 7 forward rays against road boundaries, traffic cars, and trams, combined with vehicle speed, sin/cos bearing to the next waypoint, and the distance and phase of the next signal.

04 Brain

12-14-2 NETWORK

Twelve normalized telemetry inputs propagate through a [12, 14, 2] feed-forward network with tanh activations. Two outputs drive speed-limited bicycle steering and realistic acceleration/braking.

05 Evolution

GENETIC SELECTION

Fitness scores metres progressed along the A* route plus arrival bonuses and rule penalties. The best drivers clone and mutate into the next generation, auto-advancing scenarios once arrival success reaches ≥70%.
