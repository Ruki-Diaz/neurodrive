HOW EVOLUTION drives

01

5-Ray Lidar Perception

Each vehicle casts 5 forward sensor rays across a fan angle, computing exact line-segment intersections against highway borders and traffic obstacles to return normalized proximity values from 0 to 1.

02

[5, 6, 4] Neural Brain

Sensor readings feed directly into a 3-layer feed-forward network with tanh activations. Four output neurons actuate throttle, left steering, right steering, and reverse in real time.

03

Genetic Mutation

When a generation crashes, the furthest-traveling car is crowned leader. Its weights and biases are cloned into the next cohort and perturbed by your chosen mutation rate.
